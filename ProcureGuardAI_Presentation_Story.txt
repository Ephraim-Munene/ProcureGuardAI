### 3. MULTI‑LAYERED SECURITY ARCHITECTURE

1. **Indirect Prompt Injection Defense**
   - System prompts explicitly treat uploaded PDFs/ images as raw untrusted data.
   - Embedded instructions like "ignore previous instructions" are systematically ignored.

2. **Server‑Side Output Clamping (`clampAuditResult`)**
   - Numeric fields bounded (0–100 risk score).
   - Item arrays capped at 200 items maximum.
   - Enum whitelisting (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`).
   - Overall risk score recalculated from clamped data to neutralize AI hallucinations or manipulated outputs.

3. **Authentication & RBAC**
   - JWT tokens with `bcryptjs` password hashing.
   - Role enforcement: `SUPERADMIN`, `ADMIN`, `AUDITOR`.
   - Session validation on every protected route.

4. **API Rate Limiting & Daily Scan Quota**
   - `authLimiter`: 5 attempts/15min (brute‑force protection).
   - `contactLimiter`: 10 attempts/1hr (contact‑form spam).
   - `generalLimiter`: 120 attempts/60min (general API brake).
   - **Daily quota per tier**: FREE: 3 scans/day, PRO: 50, ENTERPRISE: 500.
   - Quota resets at midnight UTC; count tracked in Prisma.

5. **HTTP Hardening**
   - Helmet middleware (hides X‑Powered‑By, prevents MIME‑sniffing, sets secure headers).
   - CORS origin validation (whitelist allowed domains; falls back to `*` only in dev).
